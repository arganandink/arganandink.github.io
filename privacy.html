ArganAndInk · Publisher app

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

ArganAndInk Publisher is a small personal tool. It publishes pins that the shop owner creates to the shop owner's own Pinterest account. It does not collect, sell or share data about anyone else.

Who we are

ArganAndInk is an independent Etsy shop based in Casablanca, Morocco, selling printable guides (arganandink.etsy.com). ArganAndInk Publisher is the shop's internal tool for posting its own content to Pinterest.

What the app does

  • Uploads images and short videos created by ArganAndInk to boards on the ArganAndInk Pinterest account.
  • Adds a title, description, alt text and a link to the shop's own Etsy listings.
  • Reads the list of boards on that same account so it can post to the right board.

Data we access

When the account owner signs in with Pinterest, the app receives an access token for that account only. With it, the app reads the account's own boards and creates pins on them. It does not read other people's pins, boards, followers or messages.

Where data is stored

The app runs on the owner's own computer. The Pinterest access token and a log of published pins are saved in a local file on that computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Pinterest's official API.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking in this app.

Keeping and deleting data

The token is kept only while the app is in use. The owner can remove access at any time in Pinterest settings under connected apps, or by deleting the local token file.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated.

Contact

For any question about this policy, send a message to ArganAndInk through Etsy at arganandink.etsy.com.
